## Description（完整介绍 — 英文在上，中文在下）

```
Touhou Maid: Sleep well is an addon for Touhou Little Maid that takes the "maid goes to bed" part seriously.
It fixes the moments where a maid cannot reach her kappa-compass sleep point, gives her a real quilt, and turns a good night into a small reward.

WHAT IT ADDS
- Sleep pathing fallback: if the distance to her sleep point has not improved for 5 seconds (stuck on a ladder, a fence, a one-block drop or a closed door), one vanilla safe-landing attempt is made, then a mod-side landing search moves her there and hands her back to vanilla's own bed task. Configurable seconds, attempts and an on/off switch.
- Lie-ins and naps: when her schedule leaves rest while she is still in bed she may stay 2-5 more in-game hours (30 % normally, 50 % in winter); a maid parked on the idle task rolls every 30 in-game minutes for a nap and walks back to bed for 2-3 hours. Per-maid switch, configurable chances and lengths.
- No work in winter: with Serene Seasons installed, a maid with the switch on is parked on the idle task during winter and is no longer sent to her work point; the list of paused tasks is editable in the GUI (a gear next to the switch opens a searchable checkbox list) or in the config.
- Campfire escape: a maid burned by a campfire walks to a safe spot 3-6 blocks away instead of standing in the flames.
- Quilts: a maid asleep in a maid bed is covered by a quilt that follows her own body shape, hangs over both sides and takes the light of the place. 16 faces (plain, cotton, synthetic, down, silk, summer, thick gingham, spring primrose check, four printed seasonal scenes, watermelon, cake, sheep, cat), 5 borders (none, white, black, striped, velvet) and 6 material presets (pad, drape, thickness), all picked from a paged wall inside the maid GUI.
- Your own picture as a quilt: drop a png or jpg into config/sleep_well/quilts, open the editor from the wall, drag a frame to choose what shows, then pick tiling or whole sheet, repeat count, width, dye tint and material. Pictures are cropped to the cloth's shape, never stretched; a saved quilt is named and joins the wall, and it travels with the world so other players see it too.
- Vigor and Drowsy: sleeping through her own rest window grants Vigor (+30 % movement speed); ending that window without enough sleep applies Drowsy (-15 %). Drowsy gets shorter the more she actually slept, wears off while she sleeps, and both effects stack instead of overwriting. A night skipped by the players counts as sleep for every maid in bed at that moment.
- 14 advancements (3 hidden) about sleeping, changing quilts, collecting faces and the two effects, plus a decoupled quilt unlock table: some faces ask for something in the world (eat 3 melon slices, craft a cake, breed a sheep, tame a cat, bonemeal a cherry sapling, own 3 maids), the four seasonal quilts ask for the season itself, and everything else - every border, every material, custom pictures - is open from the start.

REQUIREMENTS
- Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.x
- Touhou Little Maid 1.5.3 or newer (required)
- Serene Seasons 1.20.1-9.1.x (optional; only the winter features need it)
Client and server are both required in multiplayer.

CONFIG AND COMMANDS
Everything lives in config/touhou_maid_sleep_well-common.toml: fallback seconds and attempts, campfire search radius, winter task list, lie-in and nap chances and lengths, Vigor and Drowsy durations, sleep regeneration and the Drowsy decay switch.
Commands: /sleepwell nap (send the nearest maid to nap), /sleepwell status, /sleepwell wireframe on|off, /sleepwell quilt (open the quilt editor).

NOTES
- Only Touhou Little Maid's own maid beds get a quilt; vanilla beds are not covered yet.
- Nap and lie-in states are not written to the save: reloading the world counts as waking up.

《车万女仆：睡个好觉》是《车万女仆》的附属模组，把「女仆上床睡觉」这件事认真做了一遍：她走不到床边时帮她一把，给她盖一床真正的被子，再让睡得好这件事有点回报。

新增内容
- 睡觉寻路兜底：她离河童罗盘设的睡觉点连续 5 秒没有靠近（卡梯子、栅栏、一格落差、门被关），先试一次原版安全落点，不行再由本模组搜索落点把她送过去，然后交还原版上床逻辑。秒数、次数、开关都可配。
- 赖床与补觉：作息离开休息、她还赖在床上时，有概率继续睡 2～5 游戏内小时（平时 30%、冬季 50%）；停在空闲任务的女仆每 30 游戏内分钟掷一次补觉，中了就自己走回床上睡 2～3 小时。每只女仆单独开关，概率与时长可配。
- 冬天不工作：装了静谧四季且是冬季时，开着开关的女仆会挂到空闲任务、不再被送去工位；暂停的任务名单可以在界面里勾选（开关右侧齿轮）或在配置里改。
- 篝火跑开：被篝火烫到时不再站着掉血，而是走到 3～6 格外没有火的安全落点。
- 被子：女仆睡在女仆床上会盖上一床跟着她身形的被子，两侧垂下来、跟着当地光照变亮变暗。16 种被面（素色、棉、化纤、羽绒、蚕丝、夏凉、厚实格子、樱草细格、春夏秋冬四幅印花、西瓜、蛋糕、绵羊、猫咪）、5 种裹边（无、白、黑、条纹、绒）与 6 种材质预设（蓬松、挺括、厚度），全部在女仆界面的被子墙里选。
- 用自己的图做被子：把 png / jpg 丢进 config/sleep_well/quilts，从被子墙打开编辑器，拖动取景框选画面，再选平铺或整张、格数、宽窄、是否染色与材质。图片按被面比例裁剪，不会被拉伸；保存时会命名并收进被子栏，图片跟着存档走，其他玩家也能看到。
- 干劲与困倦：在自己作息内睡够再起床获得「干劲」（移速 +30%）；作息结束却没睡够获得「困倦」（移速 −15%）。困倦时长随实际睡眠量缩短，睡着时消退得更快，两种状态都改成叠加而不是覆盖；玩家睡过整夜时，床上睡着的女仆同样算睡过这一觉。
- 14 条成就（3 条隐藏），围绕睡觉、换被面、收集被面与两种状态；另有一套与成就解耦的被面解锁表：部分被面要求在世界上做点什么（吃 3 片西瓜片、合成蛋糕、繁育绵羊、驯服小猫、骨粉催熟樱花树苗、拥有 3 只女仆），四张季节被面要求在对应季节里待过，其余被面、全部裹边、全部材质与自定义被子一开始就开放。

依赖
- Minecraft 1.20.1 + Forge 47.x
- 车万女仆《Touhou Little Maid》1.5.3+（必需）
- 静谧四季《Serene Seasons》1.20.1-9.1.x（可选，仅冬季相关功能需要）
多人游戏需客户端与服务端同时安装。

配置与命令
全部配置在 config/touhou_maid_sleep_well-common.toml：兜底秒数与次数、篝火搜索半径、冬季任务名单、赖床与补觉的概率和时长、干劲与困倦时长、睡觉回血与困倦消退开关。
命令：/sleepwell nap（让最近的女仆去补觉）、/sleepwell status、/sleepwell wireframe on|off、/sleepwell quilt（打开被子编辑器）。

说明
- 只对车万女仆自带的女仆床生效，原版床暂时不盖被子。
- 补觉与赖床状态不写存档：重进世界就当作醒来。
- 许可：保留所有权利。
```

## 已知限制（可贴进 Description 或 FAQ）

- 只对车万女仆的女仆床生效，原版床不盖被子
- 补觉与赖床状态不写存档，重进世界视为醒来
- 「冬天不工作」与季节相关的概率需要静谧四季；没装时该行显示未安装并禁用
- 「拥有 3 只女仆」只统计已加载区块里的女仆
- 自定义被子每个存档最多 64 张、单张不超过 2MB
- 被面解锁对老存档同样生效：已经盖着的被面如果没满足条件会被锁上
